1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Field Complaint Monitoring (FCM) Complaints Module provides an end-to-end,
closed-loop quality assurance and defect management platform. It bridges the gap
between global distributors, field service engineers, manufacturing plants, and
cross-functional quality teams (CFT).

The module governs the entire complaint lifecycle across 5 integrated operational
stages:
  [1] Executive Radar        -> High-level trend detection & bottleneck monitoring
  [2] Central Monitor        -> Standardized complaints register & triage
  [3] CFT Quality Meetings   -> Cross-functional review & root cause deliberations
  [4] CAPA Tracking          -> Corrective & Preventive Action accountability
  [5] Kanban Pipeline        -> Agile visual execution & countermeasure rollout

3. SCREEN-BY-SCREEN FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1. SCREEN 1: RADAR (EXECUTIVE ANALYTICS COCKPIT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Role: Quality Directors, Plant Heads, and Operations Leadership.
• Business Objective: Instant macro-visibility of warranty failure rates, high-risk
  tractor models, geographical failure clusters, and ageing bottlenecks.

A. Executive KPI Summary Cards (Top Bar):
   1. Total Field Complaints : High-level volume across all active product lines.
   2. Pending Triage / RCA    : Complaints awaiting initial diagnostic investigation.
   3. Under Design Fix        : Engineering countermeasures currently in development.
   4. Field Implementation    : Countermeasures undergoing field/plant validation.
   * Interaction: Clicking any KPI card drills down to the Monitor screen.

B. Distribution Analyses:
   1. Model-wise Distribution :
      - Stacked column visualization categorized by tractor models (e.g., SOLIS 26 4WD,
        SOLIS NT 90 4WD, SOLIS NT 60 4WD, Others).
      - Color-coded status stacks:
          • Pending   : #f59e0b (Amber)
          • Design    : #6366f1 (Indigo)
          • Implement : #10b981 (Emerald Green)
          • Others    : #64748b (Slate Grey)
      - Category sub-filters (Distribution, Pending, Design, Implement, Others, Total)
        switching between stacked bar and focused model share pie charts.
   2. Country-wise Distribution :
      - Concentration breakdown across export territories (Thailand, Israel, Nepal, etc.).
   3. Ageing Analysis :
      - Standardized warranty resolution buckets:
          • 0 - 15 Days  : Fresh intake / rapid containment window
          • 16 - 30 Days : Detailed 4D/RCA investigation phase
          • 31 - 60 Days : Countermeasure design & bench testing
          • 60+ Days     : Critical backlog requiring immediate escalation
      - Distribution pie chart with percentage shares and count tooltips.

C. View Modes:
   - Graph View : Interactive Highcharts rendering with clear bottom legend, unclipped
     X-axis model labels, and shared multi-metric hover tooltips.
   - Grid View  : Tabular breakdown with direct count hyperlinks that route to Monitor.


3.2. SCREEN 2: MONITOR (CENTRAL COMPLAINTS REGISTER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Role: Quality Engineers, Service Coordinators, Field Managers.
• Business Objective: Complete tabular operational control of all complaints.

A. Streamlined 14 Core Columns:
   1. Action          : Edit, Delete, View details shortcuts.
   2. Reference No    : Standardized alphanumeric code (e.g., FIELD/2024/09/3).
                        Clickable link leading to deep 8D/PRTS complaint view.
   3. Subject         : Brief failure summary.
   4. Description     : Detailed symptom and field failure description.
   5. Department      : Responsible plant CFT function (Production, QA, Maintenance,
                        R&D, Incoming Inspection, Warehouse, Logistics).
   6. Severity        : Criticality rating (Critical, High, Medium, Low) with visual badges.
   7. CAPA            : Status indicator displaying if formal CAPA is initiated.
   8. Steps           : Problem-solving milestone progress (e.g., 9/15, 15/15).
   9. Progress        : Percentage bar indicating resolution maturity.
   10. Country        : Origin market / territory.
   11. Status         : Lifecycle state (Open, Pending, WIP, Closed).
   12. Responsibility : Assigned lead engineer.
   13. Complaint Date : Date logged from field.
   14. Due Date       : Agreed resolution SLA date.

B. Controls & Capabilities:
   - Active / Archive toggle: Filters out resolved/archived complaints from active scope.
   - Advanced Multi-Criteria Filter: Keyword, Role, Department, Status, Date Range.
   - Quick Creation: Top "+ Add Complaint" button triggering standardized intake dialog.


3.3. SCREEN 3: MEETINGS (CFT QUALITY REVIEWS & MINUTES OF MEETING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Role: Cross-Functional Teams (CFT - Quality, Manufacturing, R&D, Service).
• Business Objective: Structured review meetings to discuss open complaints, assign
  action items, record attendance, and publish binding Minutes of Meeting (MOM).

A. Outer Meeting Grid (Meeting Schedule & History):
   1. Meeting Ref : Unique meeting identifier (e.g., Meet/2025/10/01).
   2. Date        : Scheduled meeting date.
   3. Time        : Start time.
   4. Duration    : Planned duration (e.g., 45m, 1h).
   5. Complaints  : Clickable counter badge navigating to the inner meeting observation grid.
   6. CAPA        : Shortcut to review or assign CAPA actions generated in this session.
   7. Attendance  : Interactive modal tracking attendee presence (Present / Absent / Online).
   8. Agenda      : Modal detailing session objectives and target defect categories.
   9. Status      : Dedicated visual badge (Open = Green badge / Closed = Grey badge).
   10. Action     : Enter meeting execution room / Edit details / Delete.

B. Inner Meeting Dashboard (Meeting Execution Room):
   - Streamlined 12-column complaint review grid:
       Actions | Reference No | Subject | Description | Department | Country |
       Severity | CAPA | Status | Reviewed (Checkbox) | TAT | Date Logged
   - Deliberation Tools:
       • Notes Dialog    : Record specific technical feedback per complaint.
       • Remarks Dialog  : Log department commitments and temporary countermeasures.
   - Meeting Closure & Publishing:
       • "Publish Meeting" workflow prompts for closing remarks and summary.
       • On publish, the meeting transitions to "Closed", is archived in local storage,
         and persists in a read-only historical record state.


3.4. SCREEN 4: CAPA (CORRECTIVE AND PREVENTIVE ACTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Role: Quality Assurance Leads, Plant Heads, Design Engineers.
• Business Objective: Track systemic, permanent root cause fixes to prevent
  recurrence in future production batches.

A. Top Action Bar:
   - Aligned to the right side with consistent 10px spacing:
       [ Active / Archive Toggle ]  [ Filter Button ]  [ + Add CAPA Button ]

B. Filter Criteria:
   - Keyword Search    : Searches across Reference No, Failure issue, and Details.
   - Role Filter       : Shop Head, Shift Manager, QA Lead, Plant Quality Head.
   - Department Filter : Production, QA, Maintenance, R&D, Incoming Inspection.
   - Status Filter     : Open, Pending, WIP, Closed.
   - Actions           : Working "Search" (applies active filters) and "Clear" (resets).

C. CAPA Table Columns:
   1. Actions             : Edit CAPA dialog, Delete CAPA (with confirmation popup).
   2. Status              : Interactive inline status dropdown (Open, Pending, WIP, Closed).
   3. Complaint Reference : Links back to origin field complaint.
   4. Meeting Ref         : Traces the review meeting where this CAPA was commissioned.
   5. Role                : Designated owner title.
   6. Department          : Executing department.
   7. Subject             : Corrective action summary.
   8. Description         : Engineering countermeasure description.
   9. Due Date            : Target verification date.
   10. ETA                : Projected closure timeline.


3.5. SCREEN 5: KANBAN (AGILE EXECUTION PIPELINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Primary Role: Design & Manufacturing Engineers, Maintenance Task Teams.
• Business Objective: Visual task execution board to drag-and-drop complaints
  through engineering resolution phases.

A. Pipeline Columns:
   1. Pending : Initial backlog awaiting assignment and diagnostic verification.
   2. Process : Active root-cause analysis and countermeasure prototyping.
   3. Hold    : Blocked awaiting supplier parts, field trial feedback, or test bench.
   4. Closed  : Implemented, verified in production, and officially closed.

B. Card Structure & Visual Layout:
   - Compact, non-overflowing typography:
       • Title Row       : Defect subject (13px semi-bold, automatic text wrapping).
       • Metadata Row 1  : Distributor / Plant name & Department badge.
       • Metadata Row 2  : Logged date & Current status badge.
       • SLA / ETA Badge : Alarm indicator showing remaining days to target.
       • Accordion Panel : Expandable detail section showing Creator, Assignee,
                           Expedite urgency flag, and chronological notes log.
   - Drag-and-Drop:
       • Angular CDK Drag-Drop enabled.
       • Dragging a card across columns automatically updates its underlying status
         and persists the layout in localStorage.


4. REAL-WORLD COMPLIANCE & BEST PRACTICE ALIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Closed-Loop Traceability:
   Every record maintains parent-child traceability:
   Field Complaint (Monitor) -> Review Session (Meetings) -> Root Cause Fix (CAPA)
   -> Visual Execution (Kanban) -> Trend Health (Radar).

2. Domain Terminology:
   Standardized to real tractor manufacturing and quality terminology:
   - Tractor Models : SOLIS 26 4WD, SOLIS NT 90 4WD, SOLIS NT 60 4WD.
   - Defect Types   : Hydraulic Lift Pressure Drop, Transmission Slipping,
                      Radiator Airflow Restriction, Steering Cylinder Oil Leakage.
   - Departments    : Production, QA, Maintenance, R&D, Incoming Inspection.
   - Roles          : Shop Head, Shift Manager, QA Lead, Plant Quality Head.

3. Responsive & Clean UI Layouts:
   - Action bars use consistent right-aligned flexbox (`justify-content: flex-end`).
   - Tables feature clean white-space management without cutoffs or hidden columns.
   - Charts feature visible legends and responsive padding for X-axis labels.


5. CONCLUSION & READINESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The entire Complaints Module is now 100% synchronized, structurally aligned, and
fully represents real-world automotive and machinery field quality management
best practices.

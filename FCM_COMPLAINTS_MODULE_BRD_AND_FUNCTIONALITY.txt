--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The global automotive and commercial vehicle market represents over $4.1 Trillion 
in annual valuation (~90 Million passenger cars and ~25 Million commercial lorries 
sold per year worldwide). In this high-stakes environment, an unresolved field defect 
or uncontained recall costs OEMs between $50 Million and $2 Billion in warranty 
claims, brand erosion, and regulatory penalties.

The Field Complaint Monitoring (FCM) System provides an enterprise closed-loop 
quality engineering platform that seamlessly connects:

3.1. SCREEN 1: RADAR (EXECUTIVE COCKPIT)
• Role: Plant General Managers, Chief Quality Officers, Operational Directors.

5. 7D / PRTS LIFECYCLE & ARCHITECTURAL DISCIPLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 7D (Seven Disciplines) / PRTS system is the automotive OEM engineering workflow 
designed for deep technical root-cause investigation and containment. When a field 
complaint or high-severity internal audit failure requires formal containment, 
a PRTS Issue is commissioned.

The 7 Disciplines Framework:
  • D1: Form CFT & Define Problem Ownership (Author Dept, Containment Engineer)
  • D2: Comprehensive Problem Description (5W2H Automotive Diagnostic Analysis)
  • D3: Interim Containment Actions (ICA - Quarantine, Sorting at Dealership/Plant)
  • D4: Root Cause Verification (Fishbone / 5-Why, DTC Analysis, Component Bench)
  • D5: Permanent Corrective Actions (PCA Formulation & Validation Trials)
  • D6: PCA Implementation & Verification (Cut-off VIN / Serial Number Tracking)
  • D7: Systemic Prevention & Lessons Learned (FMEA Updates, SOP Control Plans)

7. OBJECTIVE AUDITS ARCHITECTURAL FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective Quality Audits represent quantifiable, verifiable engineering inspection 
gates executed along the vehicle assembly line and at End-of-Line (EOL). Unlike 
sensory reviews, objective audits evaluate exact dimensional tolerances, torque 
specifications, electrical insulation resistance, and diagnostic checkpoint pass/fail 
metrics against numerical engineering targets.

The Objective Audit Equation:
  • Audit Plan = Vehicle Model x Trim Variant x Assembly Stage x Manufacturing Plant
  • Metric Compliance = [Passed Checkpoints / Total Checkpoints] vs Demerit Deductions
  • Rating Scale = WDPV (Weighted Demerits Per Vehicle) Target Threshold (e.g., 93% WDPV)

  - Real-Time Dynamic Calculation Engine (calculateMetrics / updateData):
    * Total Checks   = Pass (Green) + Fail (Red) + Pending Checks (Grey)
    * Pass Rate (%)  = (Pass / Total) * 100
    * Drift Rate (%) = (Fail / Total) * 100

    * If LSL <= Actual Value <= USL -> Assigned Status "Pass" & Color 🟢 Green (rgba(127, 255, 127, 0.62))
    * If Actual Value < LSL or > USL -> Assigned Status "Fail" & Color 🔴 Red (rgba(255, 205, 205, 0.64))
  - Immediate 3-Way Real-Time UI Reflection:
    1. Checkpoints Data Table Reflection:
       Prepend new row at the top of values1 with Serial No, Grid Row, Grid Column, 
       Checkpoint Name, Measure, LSL, USL, Actual Value, Units, and Edit/Request action icons.
    2. 8x11 Spatial CAD Grid Reflection:
       Plots the coordinate cell at (Row, Col) in highlightedCells, lighting up the exact 
       cell on the vehicle CAD diagram in Green (Pass) or Red (Fail).
    3. Status Summary Card Reflection:
       Re-executes calculateMetrics(), immediately incrementing Total by +1 and 
       incrementing Pass or Fail count, giving auditors instant quantitative feedback.

• Additional Interactive Action Modals:
  - Save Draft & Submit actions for sign-off.
  - Checkpoint Request Icon: Opens AddCheckpointRequestComponent / AddRequestComponent for 
    direct rework dispatch to plant body/paint engineers.

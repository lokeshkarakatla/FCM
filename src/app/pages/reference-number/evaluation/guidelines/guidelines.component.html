01 FMEA OVERVIEW

Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA) is a systematic, structured approach to identify potential failure modes, their causes and effects, and prioritize risks to prevent failures and improve product and process reliability.

03 FAILURE MODE

The specific physical or functional way in which a process or product could potentially fail to satisfy specifications.

Example: Hole size out of tolerance

04 FAILURE EFFECT

The immediate and downstream impact of the failure mode on the customer, environment, or subsequent process operation.

Example: Improper fit, oil leakage, noise, customer complaint

06 SEVERITY (S)
Customer & Safety Impact Scale Rating: 1 - 10
  • 1 No Effect (Imperceptible)
  • 2-3 Minor Effect (Annoyance)
  • 4-6 Moderate (Secondary Loss)
  • 7-8 High Effect (Primary Loss)
  • 9-10 Hazardous / Regulatory
07 OCCURRENCE (O)
Failure Frequency Likelihood Rating: 1 - 10
  • 1 Remote (< 1 in 1.5M)
  • 2-3 Low (1 in 150,000)
  • 4-6 Moderate (1 in 2,000)
  • 7-8 High (1 in 50)
  • 9-10 Very High (≥ 1 in 10)
08 DETECTION (D)
Inspection & Escape Likelihood Rating: 1 - 10
  • 1 Poka-Yoke / Sensor Proofed
  • 2-3 Automated Inline CMM
  • 4-6 Gauge / Manual Test
  • 7-8 Visual Post-Process
  • 9-10 Undetectable Inline

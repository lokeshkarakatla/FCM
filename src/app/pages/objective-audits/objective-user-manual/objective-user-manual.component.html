2. Manufacturing Audit Gates & Checkpoint Allocation

A standard passenger vehicle undergoes 560 rigorous objective inspection checkpoints distributed across 6 sequential manufacturing gates:

GATE 1
Body-in-White (BIW)
85 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 95%
  • Laser brazing and spot weld shear integrity
  • Gap & flushness tolerances (A-Pillar, Doors ≤ 3.0mm)
  • Hemming flange tightness and sealant bead continuity
  • Dimensional accuracy via CMM / laser tracker
GATE 2
CED & Paint Shop
95 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 94%
  • Dry Film Thickness (DFT) ≥ 110 μm across all panels
  • Cathodic Electrodeposition (CED) corrosion dip coverage
  • Orange peel rating (Wave-Scan LW < 12, SW < 18)
  • Zero paint sags, cratering, pinholes, or dust inclusions
GATE 3
Trim, Chassis & Final (TCF)
140 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 93%
  • Class-A critical torque audits on steering and suspension
  • Wire harness routing, connector secondary lock seating
  • Instrument panel (IP) fitment and glovebox alignment
  • Brake line hydraulic flare tightness and fluid level
GATE 4
EV High Voltage / Powertrain
70 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 98%
  • Insulation resistance > 500 Ω/V on 400V/800V bus
  • High-Voltage Interlock Loop (HVIL) continuity
  • Battery coolant circuit pressure test (≤ 0.05 bar drop/min)
  • BMS CAN bus communication and cell voltage delta < 20mV
GATE 5
End-of-Line (EOL) Dyno & Shower
90 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 96%
  • High-pressure monsoon shower test (150 bar, 15 minutes)
  • Roller dynamometer brake force balance & ABS cycling
  • Headlamp beam aim and ADAS camera radar calibration
  • Exhaust gas emission audit (BS-VI OBD-II diagnostics)
GATE 6
Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI)
80 Checkpoints | Target ≥ 95%
  • Static road test & squeak-and-rattle (BSR) track audit
  • Infotainment touchscreen, e-Call, and wireless charging
  • Exterior cosmetic showroom gloss finish verification
  • Final VIN barcode scanning and digital gate release sign-off

3. Demerit Point Classification & Scoring Rules

Any checkpoint deviation from standard engineering tolerance is assigned demerit penalty points based on customer impact and safety severity:

CLASS A
50 Points
Critical Safety / Legal / Regulatory

Brake hydraulic leaks, steering knuckle torque failure, EV battery insulation failure, fuel hose leakage, airbag harness detachment. Immediate line stop & quarantine.

CLASS B
20 Points
Major Functional / Breakdown Risk

7-DCT transmission shudder, HVAC cooling failure, ADAS lane centering sensor drift, door closing effort > 1.5 N-m over spec, monsoon shower ingress. Mandatory 7D ticket within 2h.

CLASS C
5 Points
Minor Functional / High Customer Dissatisfaction

Door-to-fender panel gap mismatch 2.5-3.5mm, noticeable paint orange peel, minor rattle over cobblestones, infotainment Bluetooth lag. Rework before dispatch.

CLASS D
1 Point
Incidental Cosmetic / Appearance Imperfection

Light clearcoat scuff (buffable), interior carpet clip loose, barcode label tilted, glovebox damper slight noise. Addressed during standard PDI line.

functions Weighted Demerit Per Vehicle (WDPV) Mathematical Formula:
WDPV = ∑ [ (50 × NClass A) + (20 × NClass B) + (5 × NClass C) + (1 × NClass D) ] ÷ Total Audited Vehicles
Where (D)Met represents Daily Audit Compliance, (C)Met represents Cumulative Monthly Compliance, and (P)Met represents Plant Overall Target against the 94% quality threshold.

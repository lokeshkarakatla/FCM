2. Detailed 7D Discipline Execution Guidelines

D1
Team Formation (Cross-Functional Team - CFT)
Within 12 Hours
Assemble a cross-functional team with process expertise. Mandatory roles include: Team Champion / Sponsor, Team Leader (Quality), Process / Manufacturing Engineer, Design Engineer, and Supplier Quality Representative (SQE).
D2
Problem Description using 5W2H Framework
Within 24 Hours
Quantify the problem accurately: What (exact physical symptom), Where (geographic plant & vehicle coordinate), When (date/time, shift, VIN range), Who (detecting inspector), Why (deviation from drawing spec), How (operating mode when observed), and How Many (PPM / scrap rate / affected VIN count).
D3
Interim Containment Actions (ICA)
Within 24 Hours (MANDATORY)
Protect the customer immediately: Quarantine all suspect inventory (In-Plant, Transit, Yard, Dealerships). Establish 100% offline sort / inspection gates with certified marking. Identify and record the Clean Breakpoint VIN. Validate that ICA is 100% effective with zero escapes.
D4
Root Cause Analysis (Occurrence & Escape)
Within 4 Days
Identify two distinct failure mechanisms:
  • Root Cause of Occurrence: Why did the defect happen? Use Ishikawa 6M (Man, Machine, Material, Method, Measurement, Milieu) and 5-Why Analysis to drill down to systemic root cause.
  • Root Cause of Escape: Why did the defect pass existing inspection gates without detection? Investigate gauge R&R, sensor drift, or testing limits.
D5
Permanent Corrective Action (PCA) Selection
Within 7 Days
Select PCAs that physically eliminate the root cause. Priority: Level 1 Poka-Yoke (Mistake-Proofing fixture) > Level 2 Automated Sensor Interlock > Level 3 Procedure revision. Validate PCA on pilot batches before full-line implementation.
D6
Implement & Verify PCA Effectiveness
Within 10 Days
Deploy permanent tooling, machine software, or supplier tooling changes. Remove interim containment (D3) only after confirming 3 consecutive shifts or 500 units with zero defects and process capability Cpk ≥ 1.67.
D7
Prevent Recurrence & Systemic Institutionalization
Within 14 Days
Update engineering documents: Design FMEA (DFMEA), Process FMEA (PFMEA), Process Control Plan (PCP), and Standard Work Instructions (SWI). Perform "Read-Across" to replicate learnings across sister vehicle platforms (Fortuner, Creta, XUV700, Nexon EV).

3. Protocol Selection: PSR vs One Pager

Attribute Full Problem Solving Report (PSR) One Pager Quick Action
Applicability Criteria Class A / Critical Safety, Regulatory violations, Chronic recurring defects, or high-cost warranty concerns (> $10k). Isolated assembly line defects, Class C/D minor cosmetic findings, known failure modes with pre-defined rework procedures.
Required Disciplines Full D1 through D7 formal documentation with sign-offs. Fast-track D2 (5W2H), D3 (Immediate Containment), and D6 (Direct Fix).
Approval Hierarchy Signed by CFT Lead, Plant Quality Head, and VP Manufacturing. Signed by Line Shift Quality Supervisor.
Closure Timeline 14 calendar days maximum. 48 hours maximum.
